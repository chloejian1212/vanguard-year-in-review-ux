## Unverified or excluded material

- The SharePoint classroom presentation required a university sign-in and was not retrieved.
- Research recordings were listed; the review relied on available written records rather than viewing every recording.
- A demographic reference linked from the deck could not be independently inspected. Its education percentages and broad gender claims are not carried into the case study.
- There is no verified final-design comparative study, production deployment, business metric, accessibility audit, or financial calculation engine.

## Interpretation rules

Planning documents, later mockups, and observed prototype behavior are kept distinct. A control appearing in a mockup does not prove that a connected service works. Persona attributes do not establish population demographics. The asset grouping is not described as a validated participant card-sort study.

Financial copy, amounts, charts, and probability figures are illustrative content in the original designs. They do not represent measured usability outcomes or validated advice. No participant identities, personal financial narratives, recordings, or private research links are republished.
