## English detail-page introduction

An annual financial review can be visually clear while still leaving people uncertain about a term, a result, or their next step. For this personal academic project, I redesigned Vanguard's Year in Review with adults aged 50 and over in mind.

My process moved from moderated usability research and synthesis to audience framing, asset grouping, information architecture, wireframes, a UI style guide, and high-fidelity prototyping. The design makes navigation more explicit and combines financial information with contextual explanations, FAQ access, and a more supportive visual tone.

The result is an interactive design concept. The research concerns an earlier experience; the final redesign has not been shown to improve usability through a comparative study. Shared classroom research is acknowledged alongside my individual design work.

## 中文详情页介绍

一份年度财务报告即使看起来清楚，也可能让用户不确定术语的含义、结果变化的原因，或下一步应该做什么。在这个个人课程项目中，我以 50 岁以上用户为设计重点，重新设计 Vanguard 的年度回顾体验。

我的工作从远程可用性研究与归纳开始，逐步推进到受众分析、界面资产分组、信息架构、线框图、UI 风格指南和高保真原型。设计通过明确的导航、解释性内容、常见问题与更温和的视觉语言，帮助用户掌握阅读与操作方向。

最终交付为可交互的设计概念。现有研究针对较早的体验，尚未通过对比测试验证最终重设计的可用性提升；课堂共同研究与我的个人设计工作也分别保留归属。
