# Research summary

[Case study](CASE_STUDY.md) · [Design responses](DESIGN_DECISIONS.md)

## Research purpose

The research examined how people understood an earlier Vanguard Year in Review experience: what seemed clear, what needed explanation, and how the review connected to further questions or actions.

The individual course report documents Chloe's remote moderated session, with a listening discussion, think-aloud prompts, a walkthrough, and follow-up questions. Shared classroom materials provided additional context. This repository summarizes anonymous design themes rather than redistributing research records.

## Themes and design implications

| Theme | Implication for the redesign |
| --- | --- |
| A clean overview can still leave unanswered questions | Pair high-level results with explanations and optional detail. |
| Financial vocabulary can interrupt understanding | Provide definitions or an accessible explanation route close to the term. |
| Context matters as well as the result | Create a place for market context and explanation of changes. |
| Unclear clickability leaves the next step uncertain | Make navigation, primary actions, and links visually explicit. |
| Consistent information supports credibility | Use coherent scenario data and explain missing values. |
| Help should be easy to locate | Include visible help cues and FAQ access; consider a clear human-support path. |

These are qualitative themes used to prioritize design. They are not ranked by prevalence, and no percentage of users or improvement score is inferred from them.

## Method limitations

The participant in the individual report had difficulty with the initial remote setup. The session continued with the researcher sharing the interface and following verbal directions. That supports feedback about content and interpretation, but limits claims about independent navigation, interaction errors, and task time.

The supplied record documents a personal session and refers to shared class work. The presence of several transcript files is not treated as a verified total sample size or proof that Chloe conducted every session. The materials do not provide a standardized analysis dataset or a final-design comparative test.

The portfolio's persona and empathy map are synthesis artifacts. Their specific age, interests, and motivations are not treated as a population profile. Broad gender and education claims from the source presentation are not repeated as established findings.

## Facilitation reflection

The individual reflection emphasizes preparation: a suitable device, clear joining and screen-sharing instructions, a rehearsed session flow, and a script that supports neutral questions. It also records contributions to the classroom presentation's concluding recommendations.

AI assistance was disclosed for preparing natural English opening language, transitions, and the testing script, and for learning about the method. The research itself is not presented as AI-generated participant feedback.

## Public case-study scope

Participant names, exact personal profiles, financial histories, identifiable quotes, full transcripts, recordings, and private research links are excluded. The public account focuses on design problems and responses that can be understood without identifying a participant.
